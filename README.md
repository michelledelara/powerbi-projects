# 📊 Power BI Projects

> Power BI dashboards and Business Intelligence projects focused on transforming business data into KPIs, insights, and decision-support information.

---

## 🎯 About This Repository

This repository contains Power BI studies, dashboards, analytical exercises, and Business Intelligence projects focused on business and operational decision-making.

The objective is to develop practical skills across the complete BI workflow:

```text
BUSINESS PROBLEM
        ↓
DATA COLLECTION
        ↓
DATA CLEANING
        ↓
DATA MODELING
        ↓
DAX & METRICS
        ↓
KPIs
        ↓
DATA VISUALIZATION
        ↓
INSIGHTS
        ↓
BUSINESS DECISION
```

The focus is not only on building visually attractive dashboards, but on creating analytical solutions that help answer relevant business questions.

---

## 🛠️ Power BI Workflow

Projects in this repository may involve the following stages:

### 1. Business Understanding

Before creating a dashboard, the first step is understanding:

* the business problem
* the decision that needs to be supported
* the target audience
* the relevant metrics
* the questions the analysis should answer

---

### 2. Data Preparation

Data preparation may include:

* importing datasets
* cleaning inconsistent values
* removing duplicates
* handling missing data
* transforming columns
* standardizing formats
* creating calculated fields
* validating data quality

Power Query is used when appropriate for data transformation and preparation.

---

### 3. Data Modeling

The analytical model may include:

* fact tables
* dimension tables
* relationships
* date tables
* star schema concepts
* model optimization
* data categorization

The objective is to create a model that supports accurate and scalable analysis.

---

### 4. DAX and Business Measures

DAX is used to transform business rules into analytical measures.

Examples include:

* Total Revenue
* Total Sales
* Average Ticket
* Growth Rate
* Customer Count
* Percentage Variation
* Target Achievement
* Revenue by Category
* Revenue by Period
* Running Totals
* Period-over-Period Comparison

The focus is on understanding both the calculation and the business meaning behind each measure.

---

### 5. KPI Development

Dashboards may include KPIs related to:

* sales
* revenue
* costs
* profitability
* customers
* marketing
* operations
* performance
* efficiency
* targets
* trends

A good KPI should help answer a business question rather than simply display a number.

## 🧠 Data Storytelling

A dashboard should do more than display charts.

A useful analytical narrative can follow this structure:

```text
WHAT HAPPENED?
      ↓
WHERE DID IT HAPPEN?
      ↓
WHEN DID IT HAPPEN?
      ↓
WHAT CHANGED?
      ↓
WHY MIGHT IT HAVE CHANGED?
      ↓
WHAT REQUIRES ATTENTION?
      ↓
WHAT DECISION CAN BE SUPPORTED?
```

This approach helps transform visualizations into business insights.

---

## 🔍 Example Analytical Scenario

Imagine a dashboard showing that monthly revenue decreased by 15%.

A purely descriptive dashboard might only show the decrease.

An analytical dashboard should help investigate:

```text
Revenue ↓ 15%
      ↓
Which products?
      ↓
Which categories?
      ↓
Which customers?
      ↓
Which geographic areas?
      ↓
Which sales channels?
      ↓
Compared with which period?
      ↓
What changed?
```

The purpose of BI is to make this investigation easier and more reliable.

### Detailed Analysis

### Insights

Key observations identified from the analysis.

### Recommendations

Possible business actions based on available evidence.
